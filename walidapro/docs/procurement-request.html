One question for Legal

One free component in our software is only free if the product around it is also given away free. Ours is not. There are two ways to settle it, and we recommend the first.

Replace the component Recommended

Our engineers swap it for an equivalent with no such condition. About one day of work. No purchase and no yearly fee.

Buy a licence from Artifex

Quote-based, not published, and normally renews every year. Request one at artifex.com/licensing. Not included in the $5,000 above.

Not urgent, but do not forget it. It only matters before the software goes live. We are raising it now because it is the one item here that could become an ongoing yearly cost.
